# Interpretation notes

The currently executed source is `SDGSUICIDE.csv`, whose header identifies crude rates per 100,000 for 2000, 2005, 2010 and 2015. Charts show unweighted averages of available country rows. The linear line in `trend_plot.png` is fitted to those observed years and is not a forecast. The GDP/population correlation uses both-sex rows only and is descriptive, with no causal interpretation. Publisher, revision, licensing and unit documentation for the other committed files is incomplete; do not combine them or describe their contents as validated until that provenance is established.
